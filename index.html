Analytical Framework

How I Work With Data

A disciplined six-stage methodology ensuring business alignment, data integrity, executive clarity, and workflow efficiency.

⚡ Disciplined execution framework where Data Analytics is the core foundation and AI Automation accelerates selected workflows.
01–05: Core Analytics 06: Additional Capability
STEP 01 Core Analytics

Understand

Understand the business question and identify relevant KPIs. Clarify stakeholder success criteria and determine the analytical goals before touching the data.

STEP 02 Core Analytics

Prepare

Clean, validate, transform, and structure the data. Handle null values, anomalous dates, categorical discrepancies, and build robust relational schemas.

STEP 03 Core Analytics

Analyze

Use SQL, Python, and statistical analysis to identify patterns. Leverage window functions (LAG, RANK), CTEs, customer RFM quintiles, and trend calculations.

STEP 04 Core Analytics

Visualize

Build clear Power BI dashboards and reports. Craft executive layouts, intuitive slicers, and high-contrast KPI cards designed for effortless decision-making.

STEP 05 Core Analytics

Communicate

Translate findings into understandable business insights and recommendations. Frame data stories in terms of revenue preservation, margin growth, and risk reduction.

STEP 06 Additional Capability

Automate

Where useful, use AI and automation to reduce repetitive analytical work. Streamline recurrent briefings, database summaries, and scheduled data verification flows.
